# Behind the Scenes at Fix It Today

Fix It Today is a Singapore business built around one idea: help people make things, and keep the machines that make them working. In practice, that covers five kinds of work.

Here is what goes on behind the scenes.

## 1. Fixing 3D printers

Repair is where the name comes from. We help with faults on Bambu Lab, Prusa, Creality and other FDM printers: nozzles and extruders, AMS and filament feed problems, bed adhesion, motion, heating, wiring and print quality.

A typical repair does not start with a screwdriver. It starts with questions. Which printer is it? What exactly happens? Is there an error message? We ask customers for photos and a short video, because a ten-second clip often shows more than a long description. Then we work out the likely cause and agree the scope and price before any work starts. Replacement parts are quoted separately.

Some printers are brought to us; for others we make an on-site visit, which starts from SGD 120 for basic service and calibration. Read more on our [3D printer repair page](https://www.fixitoday.com/blog/3d-printer-repair), or email fixittoday.contact@gmail.com with your printer model, the fault, photos and a short video.

## 2. Printing parts for other people

Not everyone owns a printer, and not every part is worth designing yourself. We print parts from customers' 3D models and help with CAD design when a part needs to be drawn first. Typical jobs are prototypes, enclosures, fixtures and parts for research and business projects.

Anyone can [start a print request](https://www.fixitoday.com/prints/request): upload a model, choose a material and colour, and review the quote. We accept STL, OBJ and 3MF files. If a part needs to be drawn first, our CAD design service can help before it goes to print.



For larger projects, we also work on custom metal fabrication from CAD files or drawings (usually two to six weeks, depending on the job) and [custom electronics](https://www.fixitoday.com/electronics-prototyping) for research setups and automation projects.

## 3. Choosing and supplying filament

We have been using Lanbo filament for over nine years, and it remains one of the most popular choices for reliable, consistent FDM printing. We also stock Bambu Lab filament for customers who want the special finishes and ready-made AMS profiles, plus Marble and Wood PLA for decorative work.

Our [shop](https://www.fixitoday.com/shop) lists the current range of filament, along with a filament dehydrator and 3D printing pens. For schools and makerspaces that need many rolls, there is a bulk filament enquiry where you can mix colours and get a quote.

When customers ask which filament to use, we start with the part rather than the spool: where will it live, does it need to take a load, and how should it look?

## 4. Electronics and components

Alongside filament, our shop carries electronics and components: ESP32 and ESP8266 boards, an Arduino Uno-compatible board, the BBC micro:bit, Raspberry Pi kits, sensors, components and tools.

We also publish step-by-step guides on our blog, from choosing filament to troubleshooting common printer problems.

## 5. Workshops for schools and companies

We also run [school STEM programmes](https://www.fixitoday.com/school-programmes) and [company workshops](https://www.fixitoday.com/company-workshops) covering 3D design, 3D printing, Arduino electronics and introductory robotics.

In one programme, students combined model making, Arduino programming, electronic components and 3D-printed parts into an escape-room project. They measured panels, assembled room layouts, tested printed mechanisms and built an interactive electronics board with a display and sensor. In 3D-pen sessions, participants draw objects such as phone stands directly in the air.

You can see more on our [school projects page](https://www.fixitoday.com/blog/school-stem-collaborations).

## How we work

A few principles run through everything we do:

- **Diagnose before quoting.** We would rather ask one more question than guess.
- **Be clear about cost.** Scope and price are agreed before work starts.
- **Share what we learn.** Our guides help people fix and make things themselves.
