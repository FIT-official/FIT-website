# A Guide to Bambu Lab Filament: PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA and Support

If you print on a Bambu Lab machine, Bambu Lab's own filament is the simplest place to start. It is designed for Bambu Lab printers and the AMS (Automatic Material System), and you select the matching filament profile in Bambu Studio before slicing. That does not mean other brands will not work — they do — but it removes a lot of guesswork when you are learning.

This guide walks through the Bambu Lab filaments we list at Fix It Today, what each one is good for, and the choices you will face when you buy, such as refill versus spool.

## Refill or spool?

Several Bambu Lab filaments are sold either as a **refill** (filament only, without a spool) or **with a reusable Bambu spool**. If you already have empty Bambu spools, a refill saves money and plastic: you fit the coil onto your existing spool. If you are new to Bambu Lab or need another spool for the AMS, buy one with a spool.

On our shop, the spool is an option on the product page. For example, PLA Basic and PLA Matte are listed **from SGD 21.90**, with a reusable spool adding **SGD 4.00**.

## PETG Basic — tougher and more heat-tolerant

[Bambu Lab PETG Basic](https://www.fixitoday.com/products/bambu-lab-3d-printing-filament-1kg-petg-basic) is tougher and more heat-tolerant than PLA, with a slight flex. That makes it good for water-tight containers, clips and other practical parts. As with any PETG, expect a little more stringing than PLA and a strong grip on the build plate. **SGD 25.90.**

## ABS and ASA — for heat and outdoor use

- **ABS — from SGD 21.90.** Tough and more heat-resistant than PLA. It needs an enclosure and good ventilation because it can warp and gives off fumes.
- **ASA — SGD 33.00.** Similar to ABS to print, but the usual choice for parts exposed to sunlight and weather.

If your printer is open-frame, start with PLA or PETG before trying ABS or ASA. Our [filament types guide](https://www.fixitoday.com/blog/3d-printing-filament-types-guide) explains the trade-offs.

## PVA Support — for complex overhangs

**Bambu Lab 0.5kg PVA Support — SGD 52.90.** Bambu Lab describes PVA as a water-dissolvable support material for complex designs and overhangs, which is dissolved in water after printing and is used only for support. Bambu Lab notes that it must be dried again if it becomes soft or sticky from moisture, and that when supporting PLA the soaking water should not exceed 50°C. ## Tips for using Bambu Lab filament in the AMS

- **Use the right profile.** Select the matching Bambu filament profile in Bambu Studio before slicing.
- **Keep it dry.** Damp filament causes stringing, popping and weak layers. PVA, PETG and ASA are especially sensitive.
- **Check the feed path.** If the AMS struggles to load or retract, start with the feed path. Our [AMS filament feed troubleshooting guide](https://www.fixitoday.com/blog/bambu-ams-filament-feed-troubleshooting) covers the usual checks.
- **Refills need care.** When you fit a refill onto a spool, keep the coil tight so it does not tangle in the AMS.
