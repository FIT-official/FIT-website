The physics

Real cell: E = V + Ir. E and V are measured in volts, I in amperes and internal resistance r in ohms.

With load R: I = E/(R + r), and V = IR.

The V–I graph is straight: intercept E; gradient −r (V/A = Ω).

Potential divider: Vout = Vs × Rbottom/(Rtop + Rbottom).

An LDR's resistance decreases as light increases. An NTC thermistor's resistance decreases as temperature increases.
