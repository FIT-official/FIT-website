The physics

Beam reactions: RA + RB equals the total downward force. Moment equilibrium about A determines RB. A whole-span UDL acts through the midpoint of the span.

Bending: M(x) = RA x − wx²/2 − Σ P(x − a) for loads to the left of x. The maximum is found at a support, point load, or section with zero shear.

Crane: check the loaded crane about its front edge and the unloaded crane about its rear edge. The crane's own weight and the counterweight both act downwards. The crane and beam are treated as rigid in static equilibrium.
