The maths

Dilution: concentrate volume = total volume × (concentrate parts ÷ all parts). The remaining volume is water.

Alligation: for strengths A, B and target T, the mixing ratio A:B is |B − T| : |T − A|. This works only if T lies between the two source strengths.

Unit price = dollars ÷ quantity. Mark-up percentage compares the increased selling price with the original cost.
