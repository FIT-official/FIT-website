The maths

Linear calibration: signal = intercept + slope × concentration.

Least squares minimises Σ[signal − predicted signal]².

Unknown concentration = (unknown signal − intercept) / slope.

Limit of detection: LOD = 3.3 × residual standard deviation / slope. Residual SD uses n − 2 degrees of freedom.

For a sum, square weighted absolute uncertainties before adding. For a product or quotient, combine weighted relative uncertainties in quadrature. Inputs are treated as uncorrelated.
