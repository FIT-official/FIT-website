Relative atomic masses: H 1, C 12, O 16, Mg 24, Al 27, Cl 35.5, Ca 40, Fe 56. Gas molar volume: 24 dm³/mol at room conditions.

The chemistry

Balancing: the number of atoms of each element must be equal on both sides. Change coefficients, never subscripts.

Reacting masses: n = m / M; use the coefficient ratio to find product moles; m = nM.

Rate: initial rate ≈ initial gradient of the CO₂ volume–time graph, in cm³/s.

For CaCO₃(s) + 2HCl(aq) → CaCl₂(aq) + H₂O(l) + CO₂(g), 1 mol of CaCO₃ produces 1 mol of CO₂ if acid is sufficient.

Rate model: V(t) = V∞[1 − exp(−rt/V∞)]. The effective rate parameter changes with temperature, concentration and chip size. This is a simplified teaching model, not a measured kinetic law.
