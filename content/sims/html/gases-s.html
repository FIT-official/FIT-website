The physics

Particle model: gas particles move randomly. Collisions with container walls produce pressure.

Constant temperature: p₁V₁ = p₂V₂. Pressure decreases as volume increases.

Constant volume: p₁/T₁ = p₂/T₂. Heating makes particles move faster on average, increasing pressure.

Temperature conversion: T(K) = θ(°C) + 273.

Assumptions: ideal gas, fixed amount of gas, uniform temperature and absolute pressure.
