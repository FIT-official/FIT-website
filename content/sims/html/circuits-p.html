The physics

A bulb needs a closed circuit and conducting parts.

Metals usually let electric current flow. Plastic, dry rubber and dry wood are insulators.

More batteries can make a bulb brighter in this simple model. Do not mix batteries in real experiments.

Brightness is a relative model: bulb power grows with the square of the voltage, so 2 cells give about 4× and 3 cells about 9× the brightness of 1 cell.
