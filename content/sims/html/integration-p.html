The maths

Estimated area = number of whole squares + ½ × number of half squares.

One square is 1 cm². When the shape covers roughly half a square, count ½ cm². For this game, an estimate within 10% of the actual area counts as correct.

The true area is calculated using many tiny sample squares, not guessed from the large grid.
