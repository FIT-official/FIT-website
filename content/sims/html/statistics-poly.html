The maths
Each subgroup mean x̄ is the arithmetic average of n measured fill weights, in grams.
Known process mean μ and individual standard deviation σ give SE = σ/√n.
Centre line CL = μ.
Upper control limit UCL = μ + 3σ/√n.
Lower control limit LCL = μ − 3σ/√n.
The total control-band width is UCL − LCL = 6σ/√n.
Signal: one point strictly outside the control limits, or eight consecutive subgroup means strictly above or below the centre line.
A point exactly on a limit is not beyond it. A point on the centre line interrupts a run.
These are x̄ control limits, not product specification limits or confidence intervals.
