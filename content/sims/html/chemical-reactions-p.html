The chemistry

Physical change: the substance stays the same. Melting and dissolving are examples.

Chemical change: one or more new substances form. Examples include burning, rusting and cooking.

Signs may include a new colour, gas, heat or light, but any single sign is not always conclusive.

A physical change may absorb heat without immediately increasing temperature. The temperature values here represent simplified observations for classroom comparison.
