The maths

Reverse differentiation: if f′(x) = 2Ax + B, then f(x) = Ax² + Bx + C. Use a given point to determine C.

Definite integral: ∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx = F(b) − F(a), where F′(x) = f(x).

The definite integral gives signed area. For geometric area, split at each x-intercept and add the absolute areas. Areas are measured in units².
