The physics

Weight: W = mg. Horizontal resultant: Fnet = ma. Static friction balances a small push, up to its limiting value. Sliding friction is assumed equal to limiting friction.

Moment: M = Fd sin θ in N m. A rigid beam balances when clockwise and anticlockwise moments about the pivot are equal. Include the beam's weight acting at its centre of gravity.

Stability: a block topples when the vertical line of action of its weight passes outside the supporting base. A lower centre of gravity gives a larger critical tilt for the same base width.
