Conservation of energy

Gravitational energy becomes kinetic energy or thermal energy. The total remains constant.

The physics

Eₖ = ½mv²; Eₚ = mgh; g = 10 N/kg.

Work done against friction W = Fd, where F is in N and d in m.

Conservation: Eₖ + Eₚ + Eₜₕ = initial mgh.

Efficiency η = useful output energy ÷ input energy × 100%.

Model: point-mass cart, no rotational energy. Track friction acts only on the stated rough section. Efficiency in rough-track rounds is energy gained in the final climb divided by the initial gravitational energy.
