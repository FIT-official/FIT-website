The physics

Solid: keeps its own shape and volume.

Liquid: keeps its volume but takes the shape of its container.

Gas: fills its container and can be compressed.

Heat and temperature: heat is energy transferred because of a temperature difference. Temperature tells us how hot or cold something is. They are not the same thing.

Water mixing model: equal heat capacity, no heat lost to the surroundings. Final temperature = (mass A × temperature A + mass B × temperature B) ÷ total mass.
