The physics

Acceleration a = (v − u) ÷ t. Its unit is m/s². A decrease in velocity gives negative acceleration.

Distance = area under a speed–time graph: trapezium area = ½(u + v) × time.

Distance increases while speed is non-negative, even during deceleration.

For straight-line motion in one direction, speed and the magnitude of velocity are equal. The gradient of a distance–time graph is speed.

Each graph segment assumes constant acceleration. Changes in acceleration may be abrupt at the joins.
