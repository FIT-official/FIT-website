The physics

Speed: distance travelled per second (m/s).

Acceleration: (final velocity − initial velocity) ÷ time taken, measured in m/s².

Distance: area under a non-negative speed–time graph, including triangles and trapeziums.

Displacement: signed change in position. For this runner travelling forwards, displacement equals distance.

Distance–time gradient: instantaneous speed. Curves have changing gradients.

Each graph stage assumes constant acceleration. At stage joins, speed is continuous.
