The biology

Plants and algae are producers. They use light to make food. Animals get energy by eating plants or other animals. Follow an arrow from food towards the eater. When a predator disappears, the animals it ate may increase. Animals that ate it may decrease. This is a first-order prediction, not an exact population count.
