Higher pump efficiency means less electrical energy wasted per cubic metre lifted.

The physics

Useful hydraulic power P = ρgQH.

ρ = 1000 kg/m³; g = 9.81 m/s²; Q is volume flow rate in m³/s; H is vertical head in m.

Efficiency η = P(useful) ÷ P(input). Electrical energy E = P(input) × time.

Running cost = electrical energy (kWh) × $0.30/kWh.

Model: steady, incompressible flow; constant pump rating and efficiency; no extra pipe losses or standby consumption. Time can be set in 0.25 h increments.
