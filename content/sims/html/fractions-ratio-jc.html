The maths

AP: Tₙ = a + (n − 1)d and Sₙ = n[2a + (n − 1)d]/2.

GP: Tₙ = arⁿ⁻¹. For r ≠ 1, Sₙ = a(1 − rⁿ)/(1 − r). If |r| is less than 1, S∞ = a/(1 − r). If r = 1, Sₙ = na.

Partial fractions: 1/[(k+p)(k+q)] = A/(k+p) + B/(k+q), where A = 1/(q−p) and B = −A. Adjacent terms may cancel when summed.
