The physics

A spring stretches more when more identical weights are attached, as long as it has not passed its limit. Once overstretched, the simple pattern cannot predict its extension.

A see-saw is balanced when the two sides have equal turning effects. Putting a heavier child closer to the middle can balance a lighter child sitting farther away. We treat the board as rigid and weightless.
