The physics

Slope: normal reaction N = mg cos θ. Down-slope weight component = mg sin θ. Limiting friction is μs N; kinetic friction is μk N. Acceleration while sliding = (mg sin θ − μk N)/m.

Strings: T₁ cos θ₁ = T₂ cos θ₂, and T₁ sin θ₁ + T₂ sin θ₂ = W. Angles are from the horizontal. The knot and strings are light.

Beam: a rigid beam in equilibrium has zero net moment about its pivot. The weight of the beam acts at its centre of gravity. A negative unknown downward load means an upward force is needed instead.
