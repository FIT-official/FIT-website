The physics

Perfect gas: pV = mRT, with R = 0.287 kJ kg⁻¹ K⁻¹ for air. The heat-capacity ratio is γ = 1.4.

Isothermal: pV = constant, n = 1 and T₂ = T₁.

Reversible adiabatic: pVᵞ = constant, n = γ = 1.4, and no heat crosses the boundary.

Polytropic: pVⁿ = constant.

Boundary work: W_by = ∫p dV = (p₂V₂ − p₁V₁)/(1 − n) for n different from 1. For n = 1, W_by = p₁V₁ ln(V₂/V₁).

Final temperature: T₂/T₁ = (p₂/p₁)^((n−1)/n).

Sign convention: W_by is positive for expansion and negative for compression. Compressor work input = −W_by when compressed reversibly.

Units: p in kPa, V in m³ gives work in kJ. For plotted volumes in L, divide kPa·L by 1,000 to obtain kJ.

Assumptions: fixed mass of perfect gas, constant specific heats, quasi-static reversible processes, negligible changes in kinetic and potential energy.
